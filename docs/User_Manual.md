# User Manual
## Student Dropout Risk Prediction System

### 1. Student Guide
**Dashboard:** 
- View your real-time academic standing.
- Navigate to the **My Progress** tab to see your historical performance.
**Academic Timeline:**
- View your historical progression, from account creation to latest assessments.
**AI Recommendations:**
- If you are flagged as At-Risk, the AI will generate an Intervention Plan.
- Navigate to the **Recommendations** tab to view your Daily, Weekly, and Monthly goals.
- Click "Mark Done" to complete tasks and improve your standing.

### 2. Teacher Guide
**Student Directory:** 
- View all students assigned to your classes.
- Approve new student registrations via the **Student Approvals** tab.
**Interventions Center:**
- Monitor the intervention status of at-risk students.
- View Overdue and Pending tasks.
**Risk Reports & XAI:**
- Review ML predictions for your students.
- Click "Explain Prediction" to see the precise SHAP factors (e.g., low attendance, high stress) driving the AI's decision.

### 3. Admin Guide
**Global Analytics:**
- View organization-wide risk distribution and predictive trends.
**Intervention Center:**
- Measure the efficacy of the AI Recommendation engine (Completion Rate, Success Rate, Average Improvement).
**System Health:**
- Monitor real-time backend API latency, Machine Learning service status, MongoDB uptime, and server resource usage.
